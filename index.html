Research interests

Research

Building mobile robots that read human context—not only geometry—and respond in ways that are safe, legible, and socially appropriate.

R P Image / 16:9

R—01

Socially-aware Navigation

Navigation strategies that account for pedestrian attention and social context when planning safe motion through shared spaces.

R—02

Pedestrian Attention & Behavior

Estimating attention, pose, and behavioral uncertainty to better anticipate how pedestrians will move around autonomous systems.
